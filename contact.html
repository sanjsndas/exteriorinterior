Editorial Submissions

If you are a designer, architect, or homeowner with a project you would like us to consider for publication, please send a brief description and a few high-resolution images. We review all submissions but cannot guarantee coverage.

Please include:

  • Project name and location
  • Architect / designer credits
  • 5–10 high-resolution images (JPEG or PNG)
  • A short project narrative (200–400 words)

Response Times

We receive a high volume of correspondence. While we read every message, we are not always able to respond individually. General inquiries are typically answered within 3–5 business days. Editorial submissions may take longer to review.

If your matter is urgent, please indicate so in the subject line of your email.

Submission Guidelines

We are always looking for thoughtful, well-researched stories about residential architecture, interior design, and the built environment. Before submitting, please review our guidelines below.

01

Original Work

We only consider original, unpublished work. If your piece has appeared elsewhere — including personal blogs or social media — please let us know. Simultaneous submissions are acceptable, but please notify us immediately if your work is accepted elsewhere.

02

Depth Over Trend

We prioritize substance over spectacle. We are less interested in trend pieces and more interested in essays that explore the why behind design decisions — the history, the material science, the human experience.

03

Visual Quality

If your submission includes photography, please ensure images are high-resolution (at least 2000px on the long edge), properly credited, and licensed for publication. We do not use AI-generated imagery for editorial features.
