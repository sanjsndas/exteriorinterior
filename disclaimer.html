1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, tablet, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide website owners with useful information about how their site is being used.

Cookies typically contain:

  • The name of the website that set the cookie.
  • The lifetime (expiry date) of the cookie.
  • A unique value or identifier, usually a randomly generated number.

Cookies do not typically contain information that directly identifies you, but they may be linked to personal data we hold about you. For more information about how we handle personal data, please see our Privacy Policy.

In addition to cookies, we may use other similar technologies such as web beacons (also called "pixel tags" or "clear GIFs"), local storage, and session storage. These technologies function similarly to cookies and are covered by this policy.

Important: Cookies themselves do not damage your device or give us access to your personal files. They are a standard part of how most modern websites operate.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following categories of cookies on our website:

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the basic functioning of our website. Without them, certain features — such as remembering your cookie preferences or navigating between pages — would not work properly.

  • Purpose: Site functionality, security, and preference storage.
  • Examples: Cookie consent status, session identifiers, load-balancing cookies.
  • Lifespan: Typically session-based or up to 12 months.

Because these cookies are essential, they cannot be disabled through our site. You can block them through your browser settings, but doing so may affect site functionality.

Analytics & Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting information in an anonymous or aggregated form. They allow us to count visits, identify popular pages, and see how users move around the site.

  • Purpose: Improving site performance and understanding visitor behavior.
  • Examples: Page views, time on page, referral sources, error tracking.
  • Lifespan: Typically up to 24 months.

We configure our analytics tools to respect your privacy — for example, by anonymizing IP addresses where possible. We do not use analytics cookies to identify you personally.

Functionality & Preference Cookies

These cookies allow our website to remember choices you make and provide enhanced, more personalized features. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages.

  • Purpose: Remembering your preferences and settings.
  • Examples: Language preference, display settings, region selection.
  • Lifespan: Typically up to 12 months.

If you do not allow these cookies, some or all of these features may not function properly.

Advertising & Targeting Cookies

We do not currently use advertising or targeting cookies on our website. We do not serve targeted advertisements, and we do not allow third-party advertisers to place cookies on our site for behavioral tracking or profiling purposes.

Our commitment: We keep our cookie usage minimal and respectful. We do not sell cookie data, and we do not permit third parties to use cookies on our site for advertising purposes.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our website may be set by third-party service providers that help us operate and improve the site. These providers may have access to certain information collected through their cookies, but they are contractually obligated to use it only for the purposes we specify.

Service Providers We May Use

  • Analytics providers — privacy-respecting tools that help us understand aggregate site usage.
  • Content delivery networks (CDNs) — services that help deliver images, fonts, and other assets efficiently. These may set performance-related cookies.
  • Embedded content — if we embed content from platforms such as video hosts, those platforms may set their own cookies. We do not control these cookies.

We encourage you to review the privacy and cookie policies of any third-party services you encounter through our site. We are not responsible for the cookie practices of external websites.

For a current list of the third-party services we use, please contact us using the details in the "Contact Us" section below.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have several options for managing cookies on our website:

  • Cookie consent tool — where available, you can use our on-site consent banner or preferences panel to accept or reject non-essential cookies.
  • Browser settings — you can set your browser to block or delete cookies. See the "Browser Controls" section below for guidance.
  • Opt-out links — some third-party analytics providers offer dedicated opt-out mechanisms. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for details.

Please note that if you disable or delete cookies, you may need to re-enter your preferences each time you visit, and some parts of the website may not function as intended.

5. Browser Controls

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. The exact steps vary depending on your browser and device. Here are links to cookie management instructions for some common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

For other browsers, please consult the documentation provided by the browser manufacturer. You can also find more general information about cookies and how to manage them at www.aboutcookies.org or www.allaboutcookies.org.

To opt out of being tracked by Google Analytics across all websites, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that sends a signal to websites indicating that you do not wish to be tracked. There is currently no universally accepted standard for how websites should respond to DNT signals.

Because we do not engage in cross-site tracking or behavioral advertising, our website does not currently respond differently to DNT signals. We do, however, respect your choices regarding analytics and non-essential cookies through our consent tool and browser controls.

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or our practices. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
  • Provide a prominent notice on our website where appropriate.
  • Where required by law, seek your consent to the changes.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies. Your continued use of the site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
