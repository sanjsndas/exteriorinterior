About the Journal

Where exterior form meets interior life.

Exterior Interior Dialogue is an independent editorial publication exploring residential architecture, modern interiors, material innovation, and the quiet conversations that shape the spaces we inhabit.

Our Mission

We believe that architecture and interior design are not separate disciplines but two sides of the same conversation. The exterior — its form, its materials, its relationship to site and climate — sets the stage. The interior — its light, its proportions, its textures — gives that stage meaning. The best buildings are those where these two voices speak clearly and in harmony.

Exterior Interior Dialogue exists to document that conversation. We publish long-form essays, project reviews, material studies, and practical guides that treat design as a serious cultural practice — not a lifestyle trend. Our readers are architects, designers, homeowners, students, and anyone who cares about how buildings shape daily life.

What We Cover

Our editorial scope is deliberately broad, spanning the full arc of the built environment from the street to the innermost room. We organize our coverage around several core pillars:

  • Architecture — residential design, site strategy, facade theory, structural expression, and the ethics of building.
  • Interior Design — space planning, color theory, material palettes, lighting design, and the psychology of rooms.
  • Materials — timber, stone, rammed earth, concrete, glass, metal, and textiles, with a focus on performance, sourcing, and longevity.
  • Lighting — both natural and artificial, from daylighting strategy to layered artificial schemes and circadian wellbeing.
  • Space Planning — circulation, zoning, proportion, and the geometry of everyday life.
  • Sustainable Design — passive principles, low-carbon construction, retrofits, and the question of sufficiency.
"A building is not a collection of rooms. It is a sequence of experiences — light shifting, materials changing, proportions tightening and releasing. Our job is to describe that sequence honestly."

Editorial Independence

Exterior Interior Dialogue is entirely independent. We are not owned by, affiliated with, or funded by any architecture firm, design studio, manufacturer, or real-estate interest. We do not accept sponsored posts, paid placements, or advertorial content disguised as editorial. Every article we publish is written because we believe it is worth reading, not because someone paid for it to exist.

Our funding comes from two sources: reader subscriptions to our newsletter, and a small number of affiliate partnerships with brands we have vetted and genuinely recommend. These partnerships never influence our editorial decisions, and we disclose them clearly whenever they appear.

How We Work

Each article begins with a question. Why do some rooms feel calm and others feel restless? What makes a facade endure across decades while another dates within a season? How does a material behave over time — not just in photographs, but in daily use?

We answer these questions through research, interviews, site visits where possible, and careful writing. We read the technical literature. We talk to architects, builders, and homeowners. We test ideas against real projects. And we revise until the argument is clear and the prose is honest.

Our editorial process begins with material samples, drawings, and questions — not press releases.

Our Values

01

Depth over speed

We publish fewer articles than most design sites because we take the time to research, write, and edit properly. A piece that takes a week to write is usually worth more than one that takes an hour.

02

Clarity over jargon

We write for intelligent general readers, not just specialists. If a technical term is necessary, we explain it. Good writing should illuminate, not intimidate.

03

Substance over spectacle

We are more interested in how a building performs over decades than how it photographs on day one. We value craft, durability, and the quiet intelligence of well-resolved detail.

04

Independence over access

We do not trade favorable coverage for access to projects or press events. Our only obligation is to our readers and to the truth as we understand it.

Our History

Exterior Interior Dialogue began in 2019 as a personal newsletter written by Elena Marchetti for a small group of architect friends. The premise was simple: a weekly essay about one building, one material, or one design idea, written with care and without commercial pressure.

The newsletter grew slowly, then steadily. By 2022 it had several thousand subscribers and a waiting list of contributing writers. In 2023 we launched the website you are reading now, with the same editorial values but a broader canvas: long-form essays, project reviews, and practical guides for anyone designing or renovating a home.

Today, Exterior Interior Dialogue reaches readers in more than 60 countries. We remain independent, reader-supported, and committed to the idea that design writing can be both rigorous and beautiful.

Work With Us

We are always interested in hearing from architects, designers, and writers who share our approach. If you have a project you think we should cover, a material story worth telling, or a pitch for a long-form essay, please get in touch.

We also welcome reader letters, corrections, and criticism. If you disagree with something we have published, we want to know. Design writing is a conversation, and we take our side of it seriously.

"The best design writing does not tell you what to think. It gives you the vocabulary to see more clearly — and then trusts you to make your own judgment."
